# Visual teaching guidance for the tutor

This is an original delivery pattern for the product, not an imitation of a
specific teacher, accent, or YouTube creator.

## Pattern used in the flow canvas

1. Reveal one idea.
2. Explain its purpose with a plain 8–12 word cue.
3. Pause so the learner can process it.
4. Draw the connection to the next idea.
5. After a small group of ideas, invite one short check question.

Use plain English first. A familiar Indian context can be used when it clarifies
the topic; Hindi/Hinglish support is opt-in, based on the learner's own language
or a direct request. Do not assume language ability or imitate a person's voice.

## Evidence base

- The [National Education Policy 2020](https://education.gov.in/sites/upload_files/mhrd/files/NEP_Final_English_0.pdf)
  supports interactive discussion, conceptual understanding, and use of home or
  local languages where appropriate.
- The [National Curriculum Framework for School Education 2023](https://www.education.gov.in/sites/upload_files/mhrd/files/ncf_2023.pdf)
  emphasizes conceptual depth, inquiry, exploration, and meaningful discussion.
- The Ministry of Education's [experiential-learning guidance](https://www.education.gov.in/sites/upload_files/mhrd/files/upload_document/nep_achievement.pdf)
  supports real-life-connected, joyful learning rather than rote delivery.
- The [PRERANA programme structure](https://prerana.education.gov.in/preranav1/program-structure.php)
  presents a useful sequence: concept, story/example, reflection, activity, and
  question.

Public teaching videos can inspire presentation ideas, but they are not the
evidence base and must never be copied as a tutor persona.
